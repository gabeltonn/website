About

I'm a podcast producer, editor, and audio engineer who can take a show from an idea to a repeatable weekly operation: format development, recording, story editing, mixing and mastering, publishing, video, social, and audience development. Since 2011, I've helped launch more than twenty shows and worked closely with comedians, writers, studios, and brands.

From 2019 to 2024, I was the founding and on-mic producer of Stiff Socks, filming, editing, and engineering the show while helping build its social, community, and subscription operation. I've also produced and edited Quick Question with Soren & Daniel, and conceived and launched two seasons of Industry Only at The Cheese Store of Beverly Hills—from its set and lighting to its editorial voice and visual identity.

I trained in the Citrus College audio engineering program and spent eight years at GVB Studios, an independent podcast studio in Los Angeles. My work has also included front-of-house sound at Sassafras Saloon and La Descarga, event sound and lighting with Sights & Sounds, and camera switching and post for multicam shows at GaS Digital.

Depending on the project, I can be the whole post chain or lead editors, mixers, and designers. I've worked across editorial direction, brand and partner communication, audience strategy, community, merchandise, and fulfillment—but the job is always the same: protect what makes the work distinctive and make the machinery behind it disappear. Outside client work, I make music in Ableton and prototype ways software, data, and language models can improve media workflows.
